# Watch Key Test: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-31_

Watch Key Test is an Apple Watch app that connects to your vehicle over Bluetooth Low Energy so you can lock,
unlock and open it from your watch.

## What the app can do

The app can lock and unlock the vehicle and open its trunk and frunk, so items can be retrieved. It cannot
start or drive the vehicle.

## What the app collects

**Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.** The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no
advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your watch

- **A cryptographic key** generated on your watch and held in its Secure Enclave. The private key never leaves
  the watch.
- **Your vehicle's VIN**, which you type in. It is stored on the watch only, and is used to find and talk to
  your vehicle.

You can erase both inside the app (Settings > Forget car and key). Deleting the app also removes them.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to communicate with the vehicle you pair it with. It does not scan for or record
other devices, and it does not collect location data.

## Your vehicle

Adding the watch as a key is approved by you at the vehicle with your own key card. You can remove the watch's
key from the vehicle at any time from the vehicle's own screen.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
