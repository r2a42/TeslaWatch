# WatchKeyT: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-06_

WatchKeyT is an Apple Watch app that connects to your vehicle over Bluetooth Low Energy so you can lock,
unlock and open it from your watch.

## What the app can do

The app can lock and unlock the vehicle and open its trunk and frunk, so items can be retrieved. It cannot
start or drive the vehicle.

## What the app collects

**Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.** The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no
advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your watch

- **A cryptographic key** for each key you add, generated on your watch and held in its Secure Enclave. The
  private key never leaves the watch.
- **Your vehicle's VIN and a name for each key**, which you type in. They are stored on the watch only, and are
  used to find and talk to your vehicle.
- **A short diagnostic log** of what the app did (for example "unlock ok" or "car not reachable"), kept on the
  watch and capped in size. It contains no private keys. It is shown in the app (Settings > Log) and leaves the
  watch only if you choose to share it.

You can delete a key inside the app (Settings > Keys, then choose the key and Delete), and clear the log
(Settings > Log > Clear). Deleting a key removes its private key from the watch. Deleting the app removes the
rest. The vehicle keeps trusting a deleted key until you remove it on the vehicle's own screen.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to communicate with the vehicle you pair it with. To find your vehicle it listens
for Bluetooth advertisements nearby and connects only to the one that matches your VIN; it does not record or
keep information about other devices, and it does not collect location data.

## Your vehicle

Adding the watch as a key is approved by you at the vehicle with your own key card. You can remove the watch's
key from the vehicle at any time from the vehicle's own screen.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
