WatchKeyT turns an Apple Watch into a Bluetooth door key for a compatible vehicle. It locks, unlocks and opens the trunk and frunk, so the wearer can get things in and out. It can't start or drive the vehicle.

Works without an iPhone. The app runs entirely on the watch, so it suits a family member's Apple Watch that is managed from someone else's iPhone. There's no iPhone app to install, no account to create and no phone needed for setup.

What it does
- Lock and unlock your vehicle
- Open the front and rear trunks
- Show whether the vehicle is locked

How setup works
1. Open the app on the watch and enter your vehicle's VIN.
2. Sit in the vehicle and tap Pair.
3. Tap your key card on the center console and confirm on the vehicle's screen.

Privacy
The watch creates its own key inside the Secure Enclave. Nothing is sent to us. No accounts, no tracking, no servers. You can remove the key from the vehicle at any time on the vehicle's own screen.

Good to know
- The app must be open on the watch to send a command. It is not a hands-free key and doesn't unlock automatically as you walk up.
- For retrieving items only. It has no way to start or drive the vehicle.
- Requires Bluetooth and a compatible vehicle within range.
- Independent app. Not made by, or affiliated with, any vehicle manufacturer.
