WatchKeyT turns an Apple Watch into a Bluetooth key for a compatible vehicle, for getting things in and out of it. It locks, unlocks and opens the trunk and frunk. It can't start or drive the vehicle.

Built for family watches with no iPhone. If your child wears an Apple Watch set up through Family Setup and doesn't have a phone, this app works entirely from the watch. There's no iPhone app to install, no account to create and no phone needed for setup.

What it does
- Lock and unlock your vehicle
- Open the front and rear trunks
- Show whether the vehicle is locked and whether a trunk is open
- Hold keys for more than one vehicle and swipe between them

How setup works
1. Open the app on the watch, give the key a name and enter your vehicle's VIN.
2. Sit in the vehicle and tap Pair.
3. Tap your key card on the centre console and confirm on the vehicle's screen.
4. Optional: to name the key on the vehicle, open Controls > Locks on its screen, send a command from the watch, then tap the edit button next to the key that moves to the top.

Privacy
The watch creates its own key inside the Secure Enclave. Nothing is sent to us. No accounts, no tracking, no servers. Your key can be removed from the vehicle at any time on its own screen.

Good to know
- The app must be open on the watch to send a command. It is not a hands-free key and doesn't unlock automatically as you walk up.
- For retrieving items only. It has no way to start or drive the vehicle.
- Requires Bluetooth and a compatible vehicle within range.
- Independent app. Not made by, or affiliated with, any vehicle manufacturer.
