## Goal
A standalone Apple Watch app that locks/unlocks/opens trunk and frunk of a **2021 Tesla Model Y** over
Bluetooth LE, using Tesla's Vehicle Command Protocol. The wearer has **only a watch, no iPhone**. The watch
was set up with **Family Setup** ("Apple Watch for Your Kids"): a cellular watch managed by a family
organizer's iPhone. The user also wonders whether this is a sellable niche (parents with kids who have
watches but no phones).

## Design (decided)
- BLE only, no Fleet API, no Tesla account, no servers, no developer registration with Tesla.
- The watch generates a P-256 key in its Secure Enclave. Pairing sends an unauthenticated add-key request
  over BLE; the owner approves by tapping an NFC key card on the console and confirming on the car screen.
  This mirrors `tesla-control add-key-request` in teslamotors/vehicle-command.
- Commands are AES-GCM encrypted/authenticated VCSEC messages. Foreground-only (watchOS won't run BLE
  sessions in the background). Not passive entry; it's a button.

## The core blocker: getting the app onto a Family Setup watch
- Xcode does not list the watch in Window > Devices and Simulators, even with the organizer's iPhone plugged in
  with Developer Mode on. The watch has no Developer Mode row in Settings > Privacy & Security.
- An Apple Frameworks Engineer said in Jan 2022 that Family Setup watches can't be used for development
  (https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/698831). No confirmed workaround exists in forums
  (threads 741161, 848433, 836507; 836507 is a normal-pairing case).
- TestFlight on a Family Setup watch: undocumented either way. Apple docs say TestFlight must be on the iPhone
  paired with the watch (https://testflight.apple.com/). Unanswered: forum thread 848433.
- Unlisted App Store release: unverified for Family Setup watches, and the free-app install path on these
  watches is unproven. There is no fee waiver for individuals.
- Other routes (ad hoc, Enterprise, Configurator, MDM, devicectl, Xcode Cloud, App Clips): ruled out.
- Fallback that definitely works: pair the watch normally to any iPhone (erases the watch, loses the Family
  Setup child experience). The user wants to avoid this.
- Existing third-party apps found (not confirmed on Family Setup watches): TSLA Keyfob
  (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1673692238), TCat – Watch Version (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6447512918),
  CyberKey (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6467743214). The user said they tried App Store apps and none
  worked. Still unknown: did TSLA Keyfob and TCat appear and install on the watch, and where did they fail?
